# KAGA V2 Final Known Limitations

1. Six Gardens have defensible physical footprints in executive map mode; the
   remaining official Garden knowledge stays textual until source registration
   evidence is sufficient.
2. The Crescent Building story is source-backed, but its physical footprint is
   intentionally not mapped because spatial registration remains unresolved.
3. The workers route remains the only end-to-end pathway-registered journey.
   The other five routes preserve the event-authored geometry where Rhino and
   event-map evidence cannot be reconciled confidently.
4. The experience is presentation-first for desktop resolutions. Mobile remains
   functional but is not the primary visual acceptance surface.
