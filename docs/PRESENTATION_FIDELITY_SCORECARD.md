# KAGA V2.1 PF-2 Presentation Fidelity Scorecard

## Method

PF-2 is scored against the visually relevant page family in the authoritative
132-page technical presentation. A score measures whether the interactive
surface behaves like the correct source archetype, not whether a static slide
was copied. Route, event, knowledge and interaction contracts are evaluated by
functional tests and remain frozen.

Scale: 1 = unrelated, 5 = directionally related, 8 = clearly the same family,
10 = visually indistinguishable from a native interactive source page.

## Cross-experience findings

- The four source archetypes now differ in structure, not merely palette.
- Strong contours are restricted to render/editorial surfaces; route/map and
  quiet-identity surfaces use hairlines, shallow edges or no contour.
- Source navigation bars and unrelated slide copy are cropped out of executive
  experience and identity imagery; the approved original files remain intact.
- Orange is limited to active indices and protocol emphasis.
- PF-1.1 Intro, Four Days and Masterplan compositions were not redesigned.
- At 2560×1080, visual fields widen while typography and controls remain at
  presentation scale rather than inflating mechanically.

## Remaining visual limitations

1. Offline-safe system fonts produce small macOS/Windows line-break variation.
2. Some approved source renders contain scene annotations that are part of the
   render itself; they are retained when they convey design intent rather than
   presentation chrome.
3. The invitation workflow is necessarily denser than the source identity
   pages because all six functional steps remain available in one live surface.
