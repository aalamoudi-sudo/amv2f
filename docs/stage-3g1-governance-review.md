# Stage 3G.1 Governance Review

## Requirement Classification

All 24 requirements have exactly one explicit classification:

- 15 source-backed.
- 2 founder-directed.
- 1 conflicting.
- 3 template-proposed.
- 3 missing.
- 0 superseded.

Template-proposed and missing records stay outside the proposed legal
denominator. No unsupported item is silently promoted.

## Authority Separation

Founder, client, engineering, HSE, route, internal operational approval,
external operational acceptance, and opening authorities are separate.
Ahmed's authority is limited to platform direction, product vision, and
founder acceptance. The pack does not use him as an authority fallback.

Project assignment does not grant evidence verification or approval. The
source-backed deliverable-approval process remains distinct from operational
acceptance.

## Unsupported Assignments

The employee workbook is used only to trace the approved محمد إبراهيم name
match and role label. It grants no project role. Conflicting execution
assignments remain open and block freeze.

## Separation Of Duties

The candidate governance model separates:

- Owner and responsible party.
- Evidence custodian.
- Verifier.
- Internal approver.
- External accepting authority.
- Opening authority.

Missing roles remain unknown. Future waivers require an explicit authority,
source, revision, and decision.

## Auditability

The candidate is auditable through:

- Source IDs, revisions, sizes, and SHA-256 hashes.
- Exact structured source locators.
- Requirement classifications and source traces.
- Versioned JSON Schemas.
- Canonical pack fingerprint.
- Validation history.
- Immutable revision creation.
- Before/after diffs and rollback pointer history.
- Explicit eligibility reasons and affected IDs.

This is a local candidate audit structure, not a production legal repository,
identity proof, digital signature, or approved operational baseline.
