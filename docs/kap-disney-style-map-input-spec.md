# KAP Illustrated Visitor Map Input Specification

## Current gate

`VISITOR-MAP-EDITABLE-SOURCE-MISSING`

The illustrated A–T concept masterplan and photographed map are useful references,
but they are not a finished visitor-facing map. The platform must display:

> خريطة الزائر التوضيحية لم تُسلّم بعد

No Disney-style map is fabricated in Stage 3E.4A.

## Required delivery

Provide an editable source in AI, SVG, PSD, or layered PDF form. The delivery must
include:

- Visitor-facing illustrated map composition.
- Attraction hierarchy.
- Entry and exit hierarchy.
- Accessible routes.
- Amenities and services.
- Toilets, prayer, first aid, information, food, VIP, media, and security.
- A reusable “You are here” framework.
- Walking-route hierarchy.
- Landmark illustrations.
- Arabic and English labels.
- Reusable icon library.
- Defined color system.
- Rights and ownership confirmation for every illustration, font, and icon.
- Source revision, issue date, issuer, and approval status.
- Registration to approved CAD control points.

## Registration package

The map cannot be activated spatially until the issuer provides:

1. The editable source and linked assets.
2. A versioned export with an immutable fingerprint.
3. Approved CAD control points and their stable IDs.
4. Coordinate system, units, north definition, and scale.
5. A transformation or registration record to the approved spatial baseline.
6. Named review and approval authority.
7. Rights and permitted-use statement.
8. Accessible-route and service-location authority.

## Acceptance outcome

Delivery of artwork alone does not approve geometry or visitor routes. The source
first enters candidate intake, passes fingerprint and rights checks, and is then
registered to an independently approved spatial baseline. Until that sequence is
complete, the gate remains blocked.
