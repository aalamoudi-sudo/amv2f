# Stage 3G.1 Product Operations Review

## Disposition

The candidate workspace is operationally understandable and reviewable. It is
not an operational readiness approval.

## Shift-Leader Comprehension

The first view states:

- KAP is `غير مقيمة` and `لا يمكن التحديد`.
- The `68.3%` value describes pack preparation only.
- The largest unresolved authority is the opening authority.
- The largest assignment conflict is the execution workstream.
- The next action is to assign a denominator authority and approve the legal
  requirement set.

Unknown is not shown as zero, failure, or a readiness percentage.

## Ownership And Authority

Workstream ownership, delivery responsibility, verification, internal
approval, client acceptance, and opening authority are displayed in distinct
columns and views. أحمد, ماجد قاسم, إبراهيم الغمري, and محمد إبراهيم are not
promoted beyond their documented scope.

## Actionability

An operator can:

- Filter and search requirements.
- Open the exact source locator.
- See missing owner, evidence, verification, approval, and spatial fields.
- Navigate a requirement to existing candidate spatial entities.
- Inspect unresolved scope without a fabricated marker.
- Draft a decision from a blocker without changing readiness.
- Preview a candidate edit, see its diff and eligibility impact, and roll back.

## Missing Before Live Operation

- Approved legal denominator and requirement authority.
- Assigned owners for the site-closure and conflicted execution items.
- Independent evidence-verification authority.
- Internal operational approval authority.
- Client operational acceptance authority.
- Engineering, HSE, route, and opening authorities.
- Complete evidence, verification, approval, acceptance, validity, and
  retention rules.
- Field assessments and verified evidence.
- Engineering registration and approved routes.
- Resolved execution assignment.

## Operations Risk

The primary risk is confusing a prepared contract with completed field work.
Persistent Arabic warnings, independent metrics, disabled freeze, and
`cannot-determine` posture mitigate this risk. A real pilot must test whether
operators understand these distinctions under time pressure.
