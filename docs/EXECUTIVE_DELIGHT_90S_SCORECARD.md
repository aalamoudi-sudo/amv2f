# KAGA Executive Delight - First 90 Seconds Scorecard

## Scope and truth boundary

This proof choreographs the approved Guest Journey from Event Proposal page 26. It does not alter the A-L stop sequence, journey window, durations, route geometry, canonical coordinates, registered places, or `pathProgress`. The illustrated map is a visual reading; the frozen registered route remains the timeline.

The signature proof uses stop C (`الاستقبال والعرضة السعودية`). It was selected because page 26 gives it an explicit 60-minute ceremonial role, pages 25-27 connect it to the approved Saudi Ardah visual, and the approved route has a preserved physical control anchor for the stop. The transition originates from that route anchor and does not claim an independently surveyed footprint.

## Deliberate constraints

- No sound dependency.
- No camera rotation, parallax, particles, springs, or repeated pulse loops.
- No new place, route, timing, fact, experience, or knowledge relationship.
- `استكشف` pauses the live timeline; `متابعة العرض` resumes the exact same elapsed time and route context.
- All segments score at least 9.0. The lowest score is intentionally the emotional impact of the required start hold: it prioritizes executive control and truth over spectacle.
