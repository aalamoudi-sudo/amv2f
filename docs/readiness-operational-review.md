# Readiness Operational Review

## Operator Review

- The first viewport identifies posture, main blocker, owner, evidence gap,
  authority gap, and next action without scrolling through KPI cards.
- KAP is shown as `غير مُقيّم`, never `0%`.
- Source preparation is visible but explicitly separate from operational
  readiness.
- Workstream filtering exposes the execution item and its conflict.
- Search reaches requirements, roles, blockers, source facts, and candidate
  spatial entities.
- Map selection retains the stable entity ID and warns that position is not
  operational evidence.

## Workflow Review

- Evidence submitted, verification, internal approval, client acceptance, and
  gate outcome are distinct stages.
- Expired evidence is visibly invalid.
- A critical blocker stays dominant even when coverage is high.
- Decision creation produces a local unapproved draft and leaves readiness
  unchanged.
- Local authoring requires a reason and preview before activation.
- Rollback restores the exact prior fingerprinted configuration.

## Known Operational Gaps

- No real KAP operational requirement/evidence pack exists.
- Engineering, HSE, and operational opening authorities are unassigned.
- No field completion, verified evidence, capacity, crowd, or live observation
  data is registered.
- The execution assignment conflict requires founder resolution.
- Multi-user approval and production identity are intentionally absent.

## Review Disposition

The product is suitable for founder review of the Stage 3G.0 interaction and
truth model. It is not an operational opening authorization.
