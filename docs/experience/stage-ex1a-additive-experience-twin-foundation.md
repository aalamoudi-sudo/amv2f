## Outcome

Stage EX.1A adds an Experience Projection Layer above existing governed truth:

```text
Project / Event / Venue
  + existing spatial, readiness, decision, evidence, and provenance truth
  -> candidate ExperiencePack
  -> synchronized map, scene, timeline, intent, and read-only projections
  -> deterministic local digital rehearsal
```

The layer references existing zone and entity IDs. It does not copy or own
zone state, geometry, routes, readiness, decisions, evidence, approval, or
live observations. It has no baseline activation path.

## Frozen Boundaries

- KAP remains candidate, not frozen, not activated, and not baseline.
- KAP operational readiness remains `cannot-determine`.
- Unknown attendance is not zero and source attendance is not capacity.
- The sequence is not a crowd or operational simulation.
- `ZONE-SHOW-001` remains unanchored.
- Candidate map markers remain display projections of existing normalized
  anchors, not engineering geometry.
- A render cannot become evidence or readiness.
- No JourneyStep can approve a decision, verify evidence, assign authority,
  or mutate a readiness requirement.
- No Stage 4 capability is present.

## Architecture Reviews

### CTO

- The layer is additive: new code consumes existing IDs and projections.
- `ExperienceSceneGateway` keeps scene renderers replaceable.
- Pack data, local selection, and rendering are separate; repository-backed
  storage can replace local candidate state later.
- Contracts and validation are event-agnostic and exercised by a fictional
  conference pack.
- Scene truth and operational truth remain independent.
- Panorama validation checks format, source, rights, revision, scope, and
  bindings without claiming authenticity beyond those facts.

### Experience Strategy

- All four days are selectable and source attendance remains qualified.
- Personas choose a source-backed journey; lenses only alter inspection.
- Every step carries the complete Experience Intent shape and shows missing
  values honestly.
- Content, emotion, place, and operational references are linked without
  promoting them to authority.
- Exact durations, owners, accessibility design, fallbacks, success signals,
  and many scene assets remain unproven.

### Operations

- Operators can inspect a sequence without seeing it as an approved route.
- Readiness, decisions, evidence, and owners are read-only projections.
- Missing and unresolved states remain visible during interruption or asset
  failure.
- `DailyLearningDraft` is local candidate input and cannot mutate baseline.

### Truth And Governance

- Candidate, design-approved, field-reported, verified, and live truth classes
  remain distinct.
- Every extracted KAP fact resolves to a source page and fingerprint.
- Scene availability never changes readiness or evidence status.
- Rehearsal is deterministic local navigation only.
- Missing rights, revision, scope, or source lineage block renderability.

## Deferred

- Real KAP panorama, registered GLB/GLTF, and editable visitor map.
- Backend persistence, production identity, live integrations, simulation,
  projection mapping, and physical-twin output.
