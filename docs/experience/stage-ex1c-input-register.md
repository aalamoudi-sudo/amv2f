inputs. Missing is not zero, failure, or approval.

## Explicit Non-Inputs

The following cannot satisfy this register by themselves:

- a screenshot or flattened concept map;
- a filename, modification time, or public URL;
- a manually entered distance or readiness percentage;
- a render presented as evidence;
- an unregistered local anchor;
- localStorage or browser state;
- a founder product decision presented as engineering, HSE, client, route, or
  operational approval.

Every accepted EX.1C input must retain project/event/venue scope, source
revision and fingerprint, rights/privacy status, permanent identity, and its
actual authority classification.
