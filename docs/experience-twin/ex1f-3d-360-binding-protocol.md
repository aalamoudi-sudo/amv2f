# EX.1F 3D and 360 Binding Protocol

## Mapping

Every candidate asset maps to an existing stable project, event, venue, destination and scene identity. Slots cover master native model, Web GLB, panorama, flat reference, camera, collision, navmesh, day/persona variants, day/night variants, rights, visual approval, spatial registration, engineering approval, optimization and publication.

`ZONE-SHOW-001` remains spatially unresolved and receives no fallback marker.

## Candidate Binding

1. Verify the exact source package fingerprint.
2. Validate format and dependencies.
3. Validate rights and privacy.
4. Record units, scale, origin, north and coordinate reference as supplied or unknown.
5. Produce an optimization preview without modifying the master.
6. Review the mapping and variants.
7. Record Ahmed's candidate acceptance.
8. Create an immutable candidate asset revision.
9. Atomically register the full revision through Scene Gateway.
10. Test switching, resource disposal and rollback.

Failure in any required mapping prevents the complete bind. A prior revision remains active. Assets do not establish engineering registration, routes, readiness or opening authority.

## Variants

The four days reference a shared master where possible. Visibility, furniture, signs, lighting, screen content, camera and persona starting position are variant records. Nothing activates from filename conventions alone.
