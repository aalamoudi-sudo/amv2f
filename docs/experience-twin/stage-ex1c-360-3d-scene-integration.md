## Outcome

Stage EX.1C adds a replaceable, project-scoped scene layer to the EX.1A/EX.1B
Experience Twin. The legal direction is one-way:

```text
existing governed project/event/journey/spatial truth
  -> SceneAssetRegistry
  -> ExperienceSceneGateway
  -> lazy viewer projection and local candidate authoring
```

The scene layer does not own readiness, decisions, evidence, routes, spatial
geometry, or operational approval. Scene selection is view state.
