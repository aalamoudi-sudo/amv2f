# Daily R&D Executive Consolidation

## Date

2026-07-11

## Executive Summary

The permanent charter establishes a research discipline for building toward Mayadeen Event Intelligence OS without expanding the active sprint. The immediate intellectual priority is an evidence-first Event Ontology and decision contract, not live feeds, simulation, physical calibration, or a new module.

No external benchmark capability is approved for implementation by this consolidation. The benchmark landscape is a research scope and must be independently sourced before factual competitive claims are made.

## Required Consolidation Fields

- **Most important global technology reviewed:** Palantir Ontology as a framing for objects, relationships, actions, and decisions; this is a research lens, not a claim of product superiority.
- **Strongest Mayadeen opportunity:** Event Ontology plus Spatial Decision Graph connecting location, owner, evidence, authority, action, and impact.
- **Most dangerous assumption:** Operators will adopt a richer spatial interface without measured improvement over a 2D plan, checklist, spreadsheet, or existing dashboard.
- **Highest-value recommendation:** Approve one bounded canonical decision benchmark before any advanced technology implementation.
- **Technology not recommended now:** Live camera/sensor ingestion, backend/audit system, workflow engine, simulation engine, physical calibration, and spatial-computing features.
- **Build / buy / partner insight:** Build Mayadeen-specific domain semantics and evidence contracts; buy or partner for commodity infrastructure only after an approved operational need; delay the rest.
- **Decisions required from Ahmed:** Select the first real event decision, approving authority, evidence pack owner, success measure, and permission for a bounded experiment.
- **Tomorrow's research priorities:** Source the benchmark claims, compare ontology and durable-workflow options against a simple operating procedure, and refine the first decision experiment.
- **Impact on the six-stage roadmap:** No change. The charter protects stage discipline and keeps the current sprint unchanged.
