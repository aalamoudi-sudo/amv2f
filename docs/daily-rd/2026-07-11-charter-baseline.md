# R&D Charter Baseline

## Agent

- **Role:** Lead Integrator
- **Date:** 2026-07-11

## Scope

- **Research topic:** Establishing the permanent R&D governance and benchmark workflow.
- **Global benchmark reviewed:** The ten categories in the approved R&D Charter: OpenUSD/Omniverse, Cesium/3D Tiles, Palantir Ontology, AnyLogic and crowd simulation, Metropolis/DeepStream, Cisco Spaces, AWS IoT TwinMaker, Temporal/Camunda, visionOS/ARKit/RealityKit, and MIT inFORM.
- **Why this matters now:** Research can create uncontrolled scope, inaccurate product claims, and technology commitments unless evidence, stage discipline, and Ahmed approval are explicit.

## Findings

- **Evidence:** The current documentation defines an Arabic RTL spatial decision and operational exercise interface, typed spatial entities, local demo data, baseline/scenario separation, complementary 2D/3D views, route and scenario structures, and projection presets.
- **Evidence:** The current documentation explicitly excludes live digital-twin, verified readiness, simulation, calibrated projection, backend, and live integration claims.
- **Inference:** The strongest near-term R&D foundation is not another visual layer. It is a small Event Ontology and provenance contract that makes future operational claims traceable.
- **Assumptions:** A major event operator will value measurable decision improvement more than additional visual complexity; this is unvalidated.
- **Key finding:** The benchmark categories are useful as research lenses, but none authorizes implementation or a new product stage.
- **Current Mayadeen gap:** No source-backed shared operational baseline, owner/evidence workflow, durable execution, or impact measurement exists in the current product.

## Decision Value

- **Opportunity for differentiation:** Connect spatial objects to decisions, actions, evidence, approval, and measured impact through an event-specific operating model.
- **Primary user:** Event leadership and the command-center shift lead.
- **Operational problem:** Current spatial state can focus a briefing but cannot yet prove authority, ownership, or completion.
- **Decision improved:** Whether a selected operational issue is sufficiently evidenced and owned for the next action or escalation.
- **Resulting action:** Assign, approve, brief, escalate, verify, or explicitly defer a claim.
- **Measurable outcome:** Time to produce a trusted priority list and percentage of claims with source, owner, evidence, and status.
- **Failure cost:** An unsupported readiness or route claim is mistaken for permission to operate or safe geometry.
- **Simplest alternative:** An approved 2D plan plus checklist and signed evidence pack.

## Delivery Judgment

- **Technical dependency:** Current baseline/scenario/demo semantics, stable IDs, future source/evidence contract, and an agreed owner/authority model.
- **Build / buy / partner / delay:** Build the domain contract; delay technology procurement; partner only when a specialist capability has an approved operational case.
- **Suggested roadmap stage:** Cross-stage governance; no stage advancement.
- **Reversibility:** High while the work remains documentation and a bounded research protocol; low after vendor-specific data or workflow commitments.
- **One proposed experiment:** Run one canonical event decision through 2D, 3D, list, and checklist views with the same approved reference pack, measuring time, accuracy, comprehension, and unresolved ownership.
- **Stop condition:** Stop if the richer spatial view does not improve the agreed measure or if evidence and authority cannot be supplied.

## Ahmed Decision

- **Decision required:** Select the first real event decision and approve whether a bounded 2D/3D decision benchmark may be conducted.
- **Approval status:** Not approved; research baseline only.

## Classification

**Critical now** for governance and claim discipline; not a production implementation request.
