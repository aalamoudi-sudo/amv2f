# Product Vision

Mayadeen is an Arabic-first RTL spatial decision and operational exercise interface for major events.

The current product helps operators reason spatially about zones, routes, readiness indicators, risks, and scripted exercises. It is a local foundation using temporary demo data; it is not a live digital twin, verified readiness source, simulation engine, or calibrated projection system.

The foundation supports:

- Executive situational awareness.
- Spatial selection of operational entities.
- Readiness and risk editing.
- Scenario playback.
- Route visibility and animation.
- Future model import.
- Future integrations remain outside the current product claim and scope.

The first release intentionally uses typed temporary demo data and local persistence only. Baseline operational data is a future state source, not present in this release. Supabase and external backends are deferred.

Stage 3D adds a local operational-capture architecture lab. It demonstrates how a future source record could be normalized, validated, preserved as an event, evaluated for trust, projected into canonical state, and delivered to synchronized output adapters. Every source, actor, device, evidence item, metric, and acknowledgement in that lab is deterministic simulation metadata. It is not a live integration or operational truth source.

The next permitted step is Integration Lab planning for one approved use case. Vendor purchase, live pilot, backend implementation, Stage 4 simulation, and physical operation remain outside the current product claim.
