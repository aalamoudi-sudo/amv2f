# Stage 3G.1 Strategy Review

## Differentiation

The capability goes beyond task management by binding each operational
requirement to:

- Exact source provenance.
- Project, event, venue, and stable spatial IDs.
- Ownership and separate authority layers.
- Evidence, verification, approval, and acceptance rules.
- Dependencies and eligibility gates.
- Deterministic preparation measures.
- Immutable revisions and decision context.

This creates a governed path from source to future opening decision rather than
a manually edited checklist.

## Ambiguity Reduction

The workspace makes four common ambiguities explicit:

- Unknown is not zero.
- Pack preparation is not operational readiness.
- Assigned work is not approved work.
- Candidate location is not engineering or completion truth.

The result is valuable now because it exposes exactly which decisions and
authorities are missing before assessment can legally begin.

## Unproven Value

The stage does not yet prove:

- Faster field reporting or verification.
- Reduced time to resolve blockers.
- Better evidence quality.
- Fewer late approval failures.
- More accurate opening decisions.
- Adoption by client, HSE, engineering, route, and operational authorities.
- Durable multi-user audit integrity.

## Real Pilot Measures

A future authorized pilot should measure:

- Time to identify the next responsible role.
- Time from submitted evidence to independent verification.
- Percentage of requirements with complete provenance on first submission.
- Number and age of unresolved authority or assignment conflicts.
- Rework caused by unclear evidence or acceptance criteria.
- Time to answer why readiness is unknown or blocked.
- Operator error rate between preparation and readiness.
- Agreement between map-linked scope and approved engineering records.
- Time from eligible frozen pack to authorized opening decision.

These are proposals for a future pilot. No live operation, integration,
simulation, AI recommendation, or Stage 4 implementation is authorized here.
