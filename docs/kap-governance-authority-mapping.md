# KAP Governance And Authority Mapping

## Source-Backed Structure

The approved governance source defines Mayadeen internal governance and the
Riyadh Municipality client path. Browser records use stable role and actor IDs.
Private actor identities and contact references remain outside public data.

The five stages are:

1. مخرج المسار
2. مراجعة أولية
3. اعتماد داخلي من مَيادين
4. اعتماد نهائي من العميل
5. إغلاق وتوثيق

Formal email or meeting minutes are the approval record. Verbal approval is not
final approval. The client response window is 24–48 business hours. Scope,
schedule, or cost impacts require immediate written communication and a
decision target within 24 hours. The PMO maintains a weekly decision/version
register.

## Authority Separation

- Founder product authority: platform direction, source intake, product
  behavior, and spatial truth configuration.
- Internal authority: Mayadeen deliverable review and approval.
- Client authority: client acceptance.
- Engineering authority: unassigned.
- HSE authority: unassigned.
- Operational opening authority: unassigned.
- Government/commercial authority: not inferred.

One authority never implies another.

## Escalation

Four source-backed levels are represented with target roles, target
organizations, response windows when stated, and written-record requirements.
No external notification is sent.

## Execution Conflict

The organization chart and detailed responsibility table identify different
private-source actors for the execution workstream. The platform records:

`assignmentStatus = conflicted`

No actor is silently selected and no production authority is granted. The
conflict affects the execution workstream only; unrelated assigned roles stay
source-verified.

## Privacy

The browser exposes role labels and opaque actor IDs only. It does not contain
raw names tied to contact data, phone numbers, personal email addresses, or
other direct contact details.
