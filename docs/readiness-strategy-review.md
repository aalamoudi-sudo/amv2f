# Readiness Strategy Review

## Strategic Change

Stage 3G.0 changes the product question from "what percentage is ready?" to:

"What blocks readiness now, what evidence is missing, who owns closure, who
owns approval, and what decision is next?"

This supports directors and operators without hiding one critical failure in
an average.

## Reuse

The model is suitable for exhibitions, conferences, festivals, sports,
cultural/government events, and public experiences because:

- Domains are package-configured.
- Applicability is explicit.
- Entity and workstream scope is generic.
- Authority and evidence policy are records, not component branches.
- Map linkage uses stable IDs and a replaceable adapter.
- KAP content lives in data/manifests, not platform-core conditionals.

## Product Sequencing

Stage 3G.0 establishes explanation, source-backed governance, deterministic
derivation, local authoring, migration, and founder review.

It intentionally does not implement:

- Controlled multi-user field updates.
- Production verification and approval.
- Durable legal workflow repositories.
- Live integrations.
- AI prediction or simulation.
- Stage 3G.1 or Stage 4.

## Founder Decisions Needed

1. Accept or reject the Stage 3G.0 visual and interaction model.
2. Resolve the execution workstream assignment conflict.
3. Name the authority allowed to approve the operational requirement pack.
4. Supply or approve the real KAP operational requirements and evidence
   policies.
5. Assign engineering, HSE, and operational opening authorities before any
   operational claim.
